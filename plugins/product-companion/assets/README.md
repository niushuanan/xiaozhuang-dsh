# Generated companion frames

English | [中文](README.zh.md)

The `v14/` directory contains 136 generated character frames, including the legacy portal flipbook for compatibility. Production semantic playback uses four accepted prone sheets per skin: 20 composer-lounge, 12 focus, 12 waiting, and 12 success drawings. All five V14 source sheets retain the accepted lean lower silhouette and transparent negative space, while the forearm guards are redrawn in pearl white with very pale ice-blue highlights and thin cobalt edges. Black gloves, cobalt upper-arm plates, legs, and the rest of the costume keep their original colors. Night Black derivation explicitly preserves this pale material so the guards cannot collapse back into the charcoal torso.

`v13/` contains 48 body masks and 48 fragment masks. The deterministic build first merges real character pixels from all 136 blue-skin V14 production frames, then seeds material cells only inside that union. Boots, hair tips, and garment edges dissolve first; the face remains protected until late, and the final six levels keep subtle upward-moving source-pixel fragments. The browser applies every mask directly to the active V14 character bitmap, so fragments inherit its real hair, skin, pale guards, and clothing pixels. Standard/Large and Deep Sea Blue/Night Black share the masks and fixed root dimensions; no transition identity is generated or altered. `v11/` and `v12/` remain older build artifacts and are not part of the active runtime path.

The shared identity prompt defines one clearly adult 29-year-old anime heroine with the accepted face, a tall slender frame, proportional natural anatomy, very long navy hair, a whale-tail ornament, cobalt-and-black techno tailoring, and a confident oneesan presence. A narrow sweetheart/V neckline keeps the design glamorous but non-explicit. The 5 × 4 lounge sheet keeps one composer contact line across 20 breathing, blink, hair-settle, shoulder, and hand drawings. Three 4 × 3 sheets provide prone focus, waiting, and success acting tied to real Agent state. Semantic character drawings remain on their 24 fps exposure timelines. Relocation uses no character flipbook, doorway, or standalone foam cloud: a 1040 ms material phase advances 48 masks with 28 ms neighbor fades. The unchanged character disappears before the fixed-size root switches, and arrival reuses the sequence in reverse. Night Black is derived deterministically from the same cleaned inputs so both skins retain one aligned identity.

Runtime display size remains intentionally smaller than the 384 px source canvas. The larger source keeps the face, hair, outfit, hands, and habitat contact details legible on Retina displays while the overlay remains compact. `voice-wave.svg` is Lucide's MIT-licensed `audio-lines` icon. It invokes browser microphone dictation directly and is not generated character artwork.
